# Landing page (index.html)

Mode: Experience. The tools are the work and lead from the first viewport.
Audience: Ben and friends, coming back to open a tool. Job: reach the tool in one click.
Content kept: name + one-line lead, now-playing (js/now-playing.js ids unchanged), six tools with their real descriptions, Synth Lab waveform morph (js/home.js), signal chain gear, GitHub, share-link redirect.
Removed by Ben: theme switcher, webring/88x31/link-to-me band, "Built with plain HTML" line. Rejected: the hardware rack (too much) and the quiet pale catalogue (too boring). Ben chose "same layout, more punch" (2026-11-27): dark ground, huge bold type, a strong colour per tool, more live motion in the plates.
Next surfaces: roll the system out to songx, tuner, fretboard, kana, lol, lab; each tool page takes its catalogue colour.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A loud label catalogue: every tool is a numbered release (BV 01-06) with its own colour, the huge index is the launcher, and the entry you are on floods the big plate with its colour. It refuses the dark dev-portfolio card grid, the hardware rack, and the quiet pale catalogue.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black ground (#111214), off-white ink. One saturated colour per tool, used as a full flood, never as a trim: Synth Lab #ff5a1f, SongX #f2c230, Tuner #35d07f, Fretboard #9b6bff, Kana #ff4f9a, Matchups #22c3d6. Plate art is printed in black on the flood with print grain; photos become duotones by multiply. Bricolage Grotesque 800 condensed uppercase for display, Hanken Grotesk for text. Buttons are solid colour blocks with black condensed caps.

STORY: You see Ben's name and what he is playing, then six releases set huge; hovering one turns it its colour, shows its one-liner and sweeps its plate in; one click opens it. Below: liner-note credits for the signal chain and code.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top line: BEN VOET left, now-playing right. Left half: short lead, then the six names stacked at 40-84px. Right half: the active plate as a big colour field with the live art, caption, description and a solid Open button. Synth Lab (orange) active by default.

FORM: Label catalogue, amplified ("same layout, more punch", Ben's choice after the quiet version). seed:4b443015bae8515d. Signature interaction: hover or focus on an entry recolours it, reveals its one-liner and sweeps the plate in with clip-path in the tool's colour. Motion grammar: plates are alive (waveform morph, drifting covers, needle settling, the scale playing note by note, kana flipping); everything stops under reduced motion.

TYPE: Display is Bricolage Grotesque 800 at wdth 75; Hanken Grotesk for running text.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
