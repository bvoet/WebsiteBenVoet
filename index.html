SL-1
Subtractive Synthesizer
Init
Program
Octave

Play

A–' white keys, W E T Y U O P black keys. Lit keys show the range.

Perform

Z / X octave, C / V velocity, hold Space for sustain. Click lower on a key to hit it harder.

Tweak

Drag knobs up/down, Shift for fine control, scroll wheel works too. Double-click resets a control.

Sequence

Every instrument has its own pattern and they all play together: build a DR-5 beat, switch to SL-1 and jam on top. Rec writes the notes you play into the steps.

Plug in

Hit MIDI to use a hardware keyboard: velocity, pitch bend, mod wheel and sustain pedal all work.
