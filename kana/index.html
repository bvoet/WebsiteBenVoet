Drill never ends and serves the kana you miss more often. Each one moves up a box when you get it right and drops back to zero when you don't; the pips on the chart show where it stands.

Quiz asks a fixed number of questions, scores you and lets you retry the misses.

Keys: type the romaji (Hepburn, or the common IME spellings like si, tu, sya); a correct answer goes through on its own. Enter submits or moves on, 1–4 picks a choice. Switch the font to learn the shapes as they appear in print and on screens.
